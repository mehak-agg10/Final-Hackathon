## Brand & Style

This design system establishes a safe, stigma-free, and empowering sanctuary for adolescents navigating sensitive healthcare topics. The visual tone departs entirely from sterile clinical software, adopting instead an approachable modern aesthetic defined by warmth, clarity, and patient autonomy.

### Design Movement
The interface blends contemporary soft minimalism with gentle tactile elements. Generous negative space, friendly organic radii, and muted tonal washes create a de-escalating visual landscape. Interactive elements possess subtle inner glow and pill-shaped dimensionality to feel responsive and inviting under the thumb without looking childish or patronizing.

### Emotional Goals
- **Autonomy & Respect:** Interfaces communicate transparency, agency, and privacy without clinical bureaucracy.
- **Calm & Safety:** Visual pacing reduces anxiety during health disclosures, symptom logging, and provider communications.
- **Accessible Clarity:** Crisp typographic hierarchy ensures health literacy across diverse reading levels and cognitive states.

## Colors

The color palette centers on soft lavender, sage mint, and dusty rose resting on a warm cream canvas. Text legibility is anchored by deep plum and slate tones to maintain strict WCAG AAA contrast compliance without the harshness of pure black.

### Palette Roles
- **Primary (`#7B68AE` - Soft Lavender):** Key focal points, primary interactive states, reassuring brand highlights, and active navigation markers.
- **Secondary (`#5B8E7D` - Sage Mint):** Wellness tracking, affirmative feedback, health stability markers, and calming structural accents.
- **Tertiary (`#C47B89` - Dusty Rose):** Exploratory self-care tags, mood indicators, and gentle personal health categories.
- **Neutral (`#2E2836` - Deep Plum Slate):** High-contrast, non-fatiguing foreground typography and crisp structural dividers.

### Backgrounds & Surfaces
- **Canvas Base:** `#FAF8F5` (Warm Cream) provides an organic, non-glare foundation.
- **Card Surface:** `#FFFFFF` with warm undertones to lift content cleanly off the canvas.
- **Tinted Containers:** `#F2EFF9` (Lavender Tint), `#EEF5F2` (Sage Tint), and `#F9EFF1` (Rose Tint) for non-threatening categorical grouping.

### Clinical Risk & Status System
Risk communication requires clear comprehension without inducing panic:
- **Low Risk:** `#3B7A57` (Deep Emerald/Sage) text on `#E8F4EC` background.
- **Moderate Risk:** `#995D00` (Warm Amber) text on `#FEF3E2` background.
- **High Risk:** `#A33B4A` (Muted Rose Red) text on `#FCECEF` background.

## Typography

Typography balances the approachable, open geometry of **Plus Jakarta Sans** for headings and narrative body copy with the rigorous clarity of **Inter** for forms, badges, and operational UI labels.

### Hierarchy & Treatment
- **Headlines:** Set in Plus Jakarta Sans with tightened tracking to maintain a friendly, unified editorial presence without sacrificing authority.
- **Body:** Open counters and tall x-heights support comfortable continuous reading for health education articles and private dialogue.
- **Labels & Microcopy:** Handled by Inter to preserve legibility at micro scales (e.g., dosage schedules, triage chips, metadata stamps).

## Layout & Spacing

The layout employs a mobile-first fluid grid structured around 4 columns on mobile devices, expanding to 8 columns on tablet viewports and 12 columns on desktop panels.

### Rhythm & Density
- **Thumb-Zone Architecture:** Primary action items, confirmations, and disclosure menus stay locked to bottom safe zones to enable effortless one-handed mobile use.
- **Breathing Room:** Content blocks use generous vertical margins (`space-lg` to `space-xl`) between distinct health questions or symptom categories, minimizing visual overwhelm during vulnerability-heavy flows.
- **Component Padding:** Internal card padding scales consistently using `space-md` (16px) for compact listings and `space-lg` (24px) for interactive prompts and risk alerts.

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy uses soft tonal layering combined with tinted ambient drop shadows, entirely avoiding harsh drop-shadows or stark dark outlines.

### Elevation Levels
- **Level 0 (Base Canvas):** Background tone `#FAF8F5`. No shadows.
- **Level 1 (Cards & Modules):** Pure white `#FFFFFF` surface resting on `0 2px 8px -2px rgba(46, 40, 54, 0.05), 0 1px 3px rgba(46, 40, 54, 0.03)`.
- **Level 2 (Interactive Elements & Drawers):** High-touch surfaces such as expanded accordions and bottom sheets use `0 8px 24px -4px rgba(123, 104, 174, 0.10), 0 2px 6px -1px rgba(46, 40, 54, 0.04)`.
- **Level 3 (Modals & Overlays):** Floating triage alerts and bottom dialogs use `0 16px 36px -6px rgba(46, 40, 54, 0.14)`.

### Border Cues
Borders serve as soft grounding strokes rather than structural cages. Cards employ an ultra-fine border: `1px solid rgba(46, 40, 54, 0.06)`.

## Shapes

The shape language utilizes smooth, welcoming radii that remove clinical stiffness while preserving structure and clarity.

### Radii Application
- **Input Fields & Standard Cards (`rounded-md` / 0.5rem):** Used for text inputs, multi-line notes, and nested data cards.
- **Action Modules & Containers (`rounded-lg` / 1rem):** Used for symptom logging containers, provider summary cards, and bottom sheet containers.
- **Full Pill (`rounded-full` / 9999px):** Dedicated exclusively to status badges, chips, primary action buttons, and segmented controls to emphasize physical touch targets.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Full-pill shape, background `#7B68AE`, text `#FFFFFF`, minimum height 48px for thumb comfort. Active state softens slightly via scale down (0.98) and a lavender glow.
- **Secondary:** Surface `#FAF8F5`, border `1px solid #7B68AE`, text `#7B68AE`.
- **Ghost/Tertiary:** No border, text `#7B68AE`, background transitions to `rgba(123, 104, 174, 0.08)` on press.

### Chips & Filter Pills
- **State Selection:** Unselected chips use `#FFFFFF` with a `1px solid rgba(46, 40, 54, 0.12)` border. Selected chips fill with `#F2EFF9`, border changes to `#7B68AE`, and text deepens to `#7B68AE`.
- **Pill Radius:** Always fully rounded (`rounded-full`) with 8px horizontal padding and 6px vertical padding.

### Risk & Status Badges
- Compact pill indicators with an integrated 6px circular indicator dot.
- **Low Risk:** Background `#E8F4EC`, text `#27523B`, dot `#3B7A57`.
- **Moderate Risk:** Background `#FEF3E2`, text `#7A4B00`, dot `#D97706`.
- **High Risk:** Background `#FCECEF`, text `#7E2834`, dot `#C44053`.

### Input Fields & Textareas
- **Surface:** `#FFFFFF` fill with `1px solid rgba(46, 40, 54, 0.15)` border and `rounded-md` radius.
- **Height:** Single-line inputs are 48px to prevent touch target errors.
- **Focus State:** Clean transition to a `2px solid #7B68AE` stroke with a soft 3px outer ring `rgba(123, 104, 174, 0.2)`. Never use aggressive reds for empty non-error states.
- **Labels:** Positioned outside the field in `label-md` plum slate with optional supporting helper text beneath.

### Checkboxes & Radio Buttons
- **Radio Buttons:** Circular (20px), border `2px solid rgba(46, 40, 54, 0.25)`. Checked state transforms to `#7B68AE` background with a concentric 8px white circle.
- **Checkboxes:** Rounded (6px radius), `2px` stroke. Checked state displays `#5B8E7D` background with an accessible white checkmark glyph.

### Cards
- **Health Log / Content Card:** `#FFFFFF` surface with `rounded-lg` radius and Level 1 elevation. Contains dedicated vertical room (`space-md`) between headers, body, and action links.
- **Emergency / Direct Action Card:** Tinted background in `#F9EFF1` with a `1px solid #E8C1C8` border to immediately signal urgent help without flashing high-anxiety alarms.
